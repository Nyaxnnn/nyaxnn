What it actually does

Five screens. No dashboard theater.

01

Track

One amount field, one tap on a category, done. Built for logging a purchase in the ten seconds you have between patients, not for a sit-down session.

02

Budget

Zero-based envelopes — every riyal you earn gets assigned somewhere. Unspent balances roll into next month instead of vanishing on the 1st.

03

Subscriptions

Set a charge once — monthly or yearly — and it enters itself into Track and Budget on schedule. Pause it, and it stays paused until you say otherwise.

04

Insights

Where the money actually went, split by needs, wants, and savings — not a wall of charts you have to interpret before your coffee's cold.

05

Net worth

Cash accounts tally themselves from your transactions. Investments you update by hand, whenever you happen to check them — no bank credentials required.

Why local-first

Nothing to breach, because there's nowhere for it to sit.

  • No account

    Nothing to sign up for, nothing to lose the password to.

  • No server

    Your balances, categories, and transactions live in this browser's own storage — they never leave the device.

  • No bank link

    You type the numbers in. Slower than Plaid, but nothing with read access to your accounts.

  • Yours to move

    One button exports everything as a plain file, for backup or for carrying to another device by hand.

Built for one person's actual problem: a resident's schedule leaves no room for a budgeting app that wants a sit-down session, and no appetite for another account holding financial data on a server somewhere.

So it's a ledger you can open between patients, log an expense in under ten seconds, and close — with the accounting done automatically underneath.
